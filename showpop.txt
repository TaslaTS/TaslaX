**For RailOne Users**

RailOne login ab bina token ke kiya ja sakta hai. Aapko normally apne scheduled time par login karna hai.

**Important:** RailOne APK open karne se pehle hamesha **Clear Data** aur **Clear Cache** zaroor karein. Aap APK ka Clear Data aur Clear Cache pehle kabhi bhi kar sakte hain, lekin APK ko open **58 ya uske baad hi** karna hai.

Jab aapka pair **“Wait for PG”** par 58 par jaye, tab RailOne APK mobile me open karein. Wahan apni ID enter karein aur **Connect** kar dein. Dhyan rahe, 10 ya 11 baje se pehle APK open aur connected ho jana chahiye.

Finally, jab dono RailOne pairs **“Countdown”** par aa jayein, tab **“Fix IP Error”** par click karein. Jaise hi APK restart ho, turant apni ID enter karke **Connect** kar dein.

Is process ko follow karne se IP block nahi hoga.
