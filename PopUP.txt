RailOne Captcha Issue Fix (One Time Step)
Jin users ko RailOne mein captcha login issue aa raha hai, unse request hai ke ye steps follow karein:
AppData > Local mein jaake "ms-playwright" folder delete karein.
Update ke baad Data > Install RailOne Driver run karein.
Iske baad captcha browser easily open hoga, aur koi error nahi aayega.

Important Reminder
New APK me apko apk Open karna hai login karna hai train search karte hue payment
tak jana hai fir waha se home par wapas aakar tab apko apna id enter karke
connection ko connect kar dena hai.
Agar ap jada form laga rahe hai to har pair ke login par apk ko close and reopen kar le.
Chahe RailOne Login ho, App ho ya SUP – agar aap Connection APK use kar rahe hain:
Pehle saare pairs ready karein.
APK 55 ya 54 par open karein aur connect karein.
Sabhi pairs ek sath login karein.
Login Karte wakt khaas khaya rakhe ki apk status connected show kar raha hai.
Kyuki APK me har 1Minute par connection auto reconnect hota hai.

Web Login ko aur perfect kia jaa raha hai jaldi hi fast karke update kar dia jayega.
