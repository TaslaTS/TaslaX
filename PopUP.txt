05 February 2026 – Important Notice

Aapke device me jo bhi purane RailOne aur IRCTC APK installed hain, un sab ko uninstall kar dein. Uske baad fresh APK ko scan karke download aur install karein.

Purane APK se generate hue tokens ab kaam nahi karenge. Software ka update hona mandatory hai. Agar aapka software auto-update nahi hua hai, to manually updater run karna zaroori hai.

APK banane ka process pehle jaisa hi rahega. Bas itni si request hai spoof ki scroll karne par neeche di gayi saari values ko bhi generate karein aur unhe randomly generate karein.

Aur zyada details ke liye hamare Telegram channel aur WhatsApp channel ko join karein.
