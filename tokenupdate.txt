Niche diye gaye steps ko dhyan se padho aur follow karo:

Sabse pehle APK open karo aur "About Me" section me jaakar "Clear App Data" button par click karo.
Phir APK dobara open karo aur "Spoof" column me jao, waha "Fix IP Issue" par click karo.

APK automatically restart ho jayega, uske baad token generate karo.
Token banate waqt dhyan rakho ki "Availability" par zyada se zyada 3-4 baar hi click karo, aur har click ke beech 2-3 second ka gap rakho.

Achha token paane ke liye hamesha token 10-15 minute pehle hi generate karo.
Old token bilkul bhi store mat karo.
