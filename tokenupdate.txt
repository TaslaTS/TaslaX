Jab bhi token banate waqt “Unable to process IP” ka error aaye, to kripya Tesla logo par click karein. Uske baad “Spoof” section me jaakar “Fix IP Issue” button par click karein, phir app ko restart karein aur token dobara banayein.

Hamesha token banane ke baad “About Me” section me jaakar “Clear App Data” karna mandatory hai.

Ek baar me sirf 8–10 tokens hi banayein. Is process me Login, Train Search, aur Availability par 3–4 baar click karein, har 2–3 second ke interval par. Beech-beech me mobile screen par kahin bhi click karte rahein, taaki device motion record ho aur token sahi tareeke se generate ho.

Subah booking ke liye tokens raat me hi bana lein. Agar aapko 30 tokens banane hain, to unhe 3 alag-alag rounds me banayein. Saare tokens server par store hote rahenge, aur jab jitne chahiye ho, utne retrieve kar sakte hain.
