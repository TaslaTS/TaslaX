Better results ke Jo Time dia gaya hai us time par token bana le aur send kar de fir jab aap software open kare us time get kar le.
Token banane se pehle **About** me jaakar **Clear App Data** karein.
Sabse pehle token **bina kisi spoof ko enable kiye** banayein.
Zyada token banane ke chakkar me apna kaam kharab na karein.
Sirf **10–15 token** hi banayein.
Availability check karte waqt **1–2 second ka gap** rakhein.
Agar aap lagataar availability par tap karenge ya kisi bhi button ko spam karenge, to token blacklisted ho sakta hai.
Isliye sab kuch araam se karein.
**Simple steps token banane ke liye:**
1. App open karein
2. **About** par click karein
3. **Clear App Data** karein
4. App ko reopen karein
5. Login karein
6. Train search karein
7. **Availability** par 5–6 baar click karein, har click ke beech **1–2 second ka interval** rakhein
8. Passenger details fill karein
9. Captcha fill karein
10. Payment me **iPay/UPI** select karein
11. Payment page par pahunchne ke baad user ID se login karein aur token send kar dein
12. Uske baad phir se **About** me jaakar **Clear App Data** karein

Agar dobara token banana ho, to is baar **dusra IP ya Wi-Fi** use karein aur **Device Identifier Spoof** enable kar lein.
Agar token generate karte wakt ip error aata hai to spoof me jakar device identifier ko enable kar de aur apk restart kar de dhyan rakhe spoof enable karne ke baad clear app data na kare only restart kare.
